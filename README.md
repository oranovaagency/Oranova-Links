# Oranova · Links

Official links page of **Oranova**, a digital agency based in Oran, Algeria.
This is the page that opens when someone taps an Oranova NFC card or scans its QR code.

**Live page:** https://oranovaagency.github.io/Oranova-Links/

## What's on the page

- WhatsApp chat (pre-written message)
- Email, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook
- Our services: websites, mobile apps, desktop software, QR menus and NFC cards
- "Save contact" (downloads a vCard) and "Share" buttons
- French and Arabic, with a language switch

## Hosting

Hosted for free with **GitHub Pages**:
Settings → Pages → Source: *Deploy from a branch* → `main` / root.

> The repository must stay named `Oranova-Links`: the address above is printed in the QR code
> and programmed in the NFC chip of every Oranova card. Renaming the repository breaks them.

## Updating

- **Phone / WhatsApp number:** change `PHONE` at the top of the `<script>` in `index.html`.
- **Texts:** French and Arabic texts are in the `T` object in the same script.
